# Development Log - 2026-09-24

## Changes

- **[sticky titles]** (#290): A long event scrolled partly out of the grid now keeps its title on the part still in view, in every grid.
  - Covered: resource timelines (horizontal: month, week, day), regular and resource time grids (week, day), and the resource week's all-day row.
  - The title slides along its bar and stops at the bar's end, never past it.
  - This matches FullCalendar: its default content puts `fc-sticky` on the title (`core/src/common/StandardEvent.tsx`), pinned `left: 0` on horizontal bars (`h-event.css`) and `top: 0` on time-grid bars (`v-event.css`).
- **[sticky insets]**: In a resource grid, the sticky resource column and header share the scroller with the events. A title at `left: 0` / `top: 0` would stick under them.
  - The new `useStickyInsets` measures the elements each grid marks with `data-sticky-inset`: the widest `left`, the tallest `top`.
  - It publishes them as `--ilamy-sticky-left` / `--ilamy-sticky-top` on the scroll viewport, and a ResizeObserver keeps them current.
  - The marked elements are the horizontal resource column, the "All day" label cell, and the view header (only while `stickyViewHeader` is on; a header that scrolls away covers nothing).
  - A regular grid's header sits outside its scroller, so it publishes `0px`.
- **[default event content]**: The title is sticky on both axes.
  - Horizontally it is `inline-block max-w-full`: a title as wide as its bar has no room to slide.
  - Its offset is the published inset plus the gap it has when unscrolled (`px-1` = 4px beside, `mt-0.5` = 2px below), so it does not read as clipped against the column.
- **[draggable event]**: The event wrapper's `truncate` became `overflow-clip text-ellipsis whitespace-nowrap`.
  - `overflow: hidden` makes the wrapper a "scrolling mechanism" (MDN, `position: sticky`), so a sticky title inside stuck to the wrapper itself and never moved. `clip` is not one.
  - Measured in Chromium: `clip` still draws the ellipsis a plain-text `renderEvent` relies on.
- **[renderEvent]**: Custom content is not made sticky, matching FullCalendar, where a custom `eventContent` replaces the default generator along with its `fc-sticky` title. A consumer opts in with the two published properties; the website docs give the pattern.
- **[e2e harness]**: New scenarios `long-events` and `long-resource-events`, and a harness-only `renderEventVariant=sticky-title` setting that renders the documented custom-renderer pattern.
