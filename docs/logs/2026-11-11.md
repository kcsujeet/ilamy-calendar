# Development Log - 2026-11-11

## Changes

- **[dayjs]**: An offset-less string read in the calendar's zone now takes the first of a time the autumn change repeats (RFC 5545 section 3.3.5, #395). `dayjs.tz()` picks between the two readings by the zone's offset TODAY (`tzOffset(+d(), timezone)`, dayjs/plugin/timezone v1.11.23), so from November to March 01:30 on 2 November 2025 in New York read as EST, the second. `readInZone` retries the reading with the offset in force a day earlier and keeps it when that offset holds at it; a skipped spring reading never passes and keeps dayjs's answer. Raised with the owner first, per the dayjs rule; the owner chose this over a recurrence-only fix in `fromFloatingDate`, because the event form and drops read wall-clock strings through the same call.
- **[tests]**: Season-pinned cases (`setSystemTime`, July and January) for repeated, skipped and neighbouring readings in New York, London and Lord Howe; the recurrence "first of a repeated time" test now runs in summer and winter; an e2e spec picks 1:30 AM on 2 November in the form with the clock in January.
- **[docs]**: `docs/timezones.md` states the repeated-reading rule for the constructor and adds a fifth test rule: pin "now" when the answer could depend on the season.
