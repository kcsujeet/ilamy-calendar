## Changes

- **[tests]**: `use-calendar-engine.test.ts` failed on a non-UTC machine (5 tests in America/Vancouver, 5 in Pacific/Auckland, 2 in Asia/Kolkata, 1 more at UTC+14/−11). Every one hard-coded a UTC-shaped expectation; none was the machine's zone leaking. The tests mount with no `timezone` prop, so by contract the machine's zone IS the calendar's, and `2025-01-15T00:00Z` really is 14 January in Vancouver. To check this, the same inputs went through the engine with `timezone: 'UTC'` on Vancouver, Kolkata and Auckland machines, and every original expectation held.
- **[tests]**: Every date in the touched tests is a full ISO instant (CLAUDE.md: never `'2025-10-13'` in tests). Tests whose subject is a calendar DAY (navigation ranges, the form's selected date, the onDateChange block) mount with `timezone: 'UTC'` through a `utcCalendar` config. An instant only names a day once a zone is chosen, and with the prop the machine's zone decides nothing. `getEventsForDateRange` "end within range" uses instants on both sides and needs no prop. The recurring-range test asserts instants instead of local dates. The two "timezone prop removed" tests compare against the machine's offset read from the native `Date`, an independent source, and also assert the instant.
- **[tests]**: A top-level `afterEach` resets `dayjs.tz.setDefault(undefined)`, because the prop writes a module-global that would otherwise outlive the test.
- **[tests]**: `should report correct range when using selectDate()` passed by luck: in Auckland, `2025-12-25T12:00Z` is the 26th, which happens to fall in the same week. It now runs under `utcCalendar` with the rest of its block.
