# Development Log - 2026-10-10

## Changes

- **[recurrence]**: Editing "All events" from an occurrence on a clock-change day no longer moves the series an hour. `withTimeOfDay` (scope "all") measured the edited time of day as milliseconds since midnight; 9 March 2025 is 23 hours long in New York, so 09:30 that day is 8.5 hours after midnight, and applying those 8.5 hours to the series' first day gave 08:30. The reverse broke too: a series starting on the change day took 9.5 hours from a normal day and landed at 10:30. It now combines the series day with the edited clock time and reads that in the calendar's zone, the way the grid reads clock time since #311.
- **[#314]**: The event-timezone work (optional `timezone` on `CalendarEvent`, TZID/VTIMEZONE export, `timezones-ical-library`) was dropped and its branch deleted at the maintainer's call: #314 is rare and low priority, and the cost (a new public field, a dependency, changes across recurrence, the editor and both exports) was not worth it yet. This fix was the one independent bug that work surfaced. #314 stays open.

- **[utils/dayjs]**: `startOf`/`endOf` no longer depend on the machine's zone. Upstream dayjs-timezone rounds the clock reading in the machine's zone and moves it back with `tz(zone, true)`, taking the offset at the wrong instant when both zones change their clocks near the same date. With a New York calendar on a Vancouver machine, 9 March 2025 started at 23:00 the day before, so `getWeekDays` stepped back to 2 March: the week header for week 11 showed 2-8 March and the week of 9 March could not be reached. 2 November started two hours late. The patch rounds the clock in UTC (no clock changes), moves the instant back by the clock time rounded off (so a reading in the repeated autumn hour stays in it), and falls back to `dayjs.tz(reading)` when a clock change lies in between. Pre-existing on `main`; found while testing PR #327 on a Vancouver machine.
- **[docs]**: `docs/timezones.md` records that the machine's zone still leaks through dayjs and that the next major **must** move to Temporal, with the measurements and the acceptance check for that migration. Testing on another machine zone: child process with `TZ`, or Playwright `timezoneId`.
- **[upstream]**: Commented on dayjs#1437 (startOf) and dayjs#2304 (local `Date` design) with machine-zone repros, verified on 1.11.23 (`dev` has no code changes since) in Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Bun.
- **[docs/agents]**: `AGENTS.md` (= `CLAUDE.md`) gains a Hard Rule: read all the docs in `docs/` before changing code; for date or time work it calls out that dayjs still reads zoned dates through the machine's zone; the Temporal migration is required in the next major, no more `dayjs.ts` patches without raising it, and date bugs are reproduced with the machine zone set. Added because the docs were only referenced in passing and sessions did not read them unprompted. `docs/timezones.md` records the polyfill decision: native Temporal covers 70.18% of users (caniuse, no Safari or iOS), so the plan is FullCalendar v7's: `temporal-polyfill` through its `fns` API.
- **[release]**: 3.2.1, a patch: fixes #321/#324, #323, #327, #328 and docs #325, #328/#329 since v3.2.0. `CHANGELOG.md` section added; GitHub release carries the same notes. Published to npm by the maintainer (`bun run release`; npm is not logged in on this machine).
