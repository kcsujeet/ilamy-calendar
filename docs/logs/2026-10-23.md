# Development Log - 2026-10-23

## Changes

- **[fix]**: A drop on the all-day strip after the grid scrolled during the drag hit nothing. Cause: dnd-kit's `Rect` adds the scroll since measurement to every edge (`core.esm.js`, `Rect`, v6.3.1) and `getScrollableAncestors` ignores `position: sticky`, so the sticky strip was measured away from where it is drawn (measured 0-48, drawn 100-148 after a 100px wheel in the resource day view) and was not a candidate. `pointerWithinLazily` now tests all-day cells (`data.allDay === true`) by their live `getBoundingClientRect()` through `pointerWithin`, and a hit there wins over the time columns.
- **[test]**: Unit: three cases in `pointer-within-lazily.test.ts`. E2E: `drag.spec.ts` "a drop on the all-day strip after the grid has scrolled": timed event onto the strip, and all-day event onto a slot, for week, resource day and resource week, scrolled before and during the drag. `settleScroll` moved to module level and returns the scrollTop.
- **[docs]**: `docs/performance.md` (hit-testing section), CHANGELOG fix line.
