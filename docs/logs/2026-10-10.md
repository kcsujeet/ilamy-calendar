# Development Log - 2026-10-10

## Changes

- **[recurrence]**: Editing "All events" from an occurrence on a clock-change day no longer moves the series an hour. `withTimeOfDay` (scope "all") measured the edited time of day as milliseconds since midnight; 9 March 2025 is 23 hours long in New York, so 09:30 that day is 8.5 hours after midnight, and applying those 8.5 hours to the series' first day gave 08:30. The reverse broke too: a series starting on the change day took 9.5 hours from a normal day and landed at 10:30. It now combines the series day with the edited clock time and reads that in the calendar's zone, the way the grid reads clock time since #311.
- **[#314]**: The event-timezone work (optional `timezone` on `CalendarEvent`, TZID/VTIMEZONE export, `timezones-ical-library`) was dropped and its branch deleted at the maintainer's call: #314 is rare and low priority, and the cost (a new public field, a dependency, changes across recurrence, the editor and both exports) was not worth it yet. This fix was the one independent bug that work surfaced. #314 stays open.
